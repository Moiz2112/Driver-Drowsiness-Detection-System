About The System

How DriveGuard Vision Works

DriveGuard Vision is a real-time driver monitoring interface that watches the live camera feed, estimates whether the eyes are open or closed, tracks fatigue signals over time, and triggers an alarm when persistent closed-eye behavior suggests drowsiness.

Purpose

This system is designed to support driver safety by identifying signs of drowsiness before they become dangerous.

It focuses on live monitoring rather than manual image upload so the interface stays practical during real-time use.

How Detection Works

The app uses face and eye localization on the webcam stream, then runs a trained eye-state model to classify each eye as open or closed.

Those per-eye results are combined into a fatigue score and live alert state.

Alarm Logic

The alarm does not fire on a normal blink. It waits for persistent closed-eye frames so short blinks are less likely to cause false alerts.

Once the eyes are detected open again, the alarm stops and the system returns to monitoring mode.

What You See Live

The homepage shows the annotated camera feed, left-eye and right-eye status, fatigue level, score, and alarm state.

Current Limitations

Accuracy still depends on lighting, face angle, camera quality, and whether the eyes are clearly visible in the frame.

Best Use

For the most reliable results, use the system with a front-facing camera, stable lighting, and a clear view of the face.
